TraceGround

Agentic video-reasoning system that turns model answers into grounded evidence traces.

  • Processes video with planning, visual grounding, OCR/ASR, temporal localization, and verifier steps.
  • Outputs reasoning traces tied to evidence: timestamps, cropped frames, clips, key frames, and transcript snippets.
  • Evaluates whether each reasoning step is relevant, complete, temporally grounded, and factually supported.
